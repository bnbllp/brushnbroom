Our Story

Exceptional Service, Grounded in Integrity.

Hi, we're Dustin and Brittany. We built Brush & Broom LLP on a simple foundation: that excellence is the result of focus, hard work, and a genuine commitment to the community. We treat every residence and commercial space with the same care and precision we bring to our own home.

Who We Are

From humble beginnings to a fresh start in Mount Vernon.

My background in the high-end construction and remodel trades—starting as a finish carpenter specializing in luxury kitchen and bathroom renovations—instilled in me an uncompromising eye for detail. This technical expertise, combined with a business degree and universal EPA certification, ensures that our operations are as professional as they are precise.

When Brittany and I established our roots in Mount Vernon, we brought with us a level of resilience and dedication that defines everything we do. We understand that a clean environment is the foundation of a well-lived life and a productive workspace. Brittany leads the cleaning. Dustin leads the painting. You can book either one, or both. Our route from Mount Vernon also covers Burlington, Sedro-Woolley, Anacortes, La Conner, and Stanwood.

On January 30, 2025, we proudly launched Brush & Broom LLP. We saw a need for genuine and honest service businesses in the Mount Vernon home services market. People are tired of being treated like a number by large franchises. They wanted someone they can trust in their home, someone who actually cares about the finished result and shows up when they say they will. Aside from many other things, that's exactly what we set out to do.

Dustin: The "Brush"

Operations & Projects

With union-honed precision and a background in home improvement trades, I lead the "brush" side of our business by handling operations, estimates, and quality control. The brush side is the painting: interiors, trim, doors, exteriors, and the prep that makes a finish last. If I would not be proud to show it as our own house, it is not finished.

Brittany: The "Broom"

Cleaning & Management

As the other half of Brush & Broom LLP, Brittany owns the "broom." She personally oversees our cleaning services, ensuring every detail matters and your home feels truly refreshed. From deep cleans to move-ins and move-outs, Brittany builds trusted client bonds and makes sure you're not just a ticket number – you're our neighbor.

Our Values

What matters most to us

Real Communication

No runaround. We give clear quotes and keep you updated throughout the process. If something changes, you will hear it from us first.

Respect for Your Home

We treat your space like our own, ensuring it is protected and left better than we found it. That is a promise, not a tagline.

Work Done Right

We do not cut corners. Whether it is a deep clean, a fresh coat of paint, or both, we focus on quality because your home deserves it.

Giving Back

More than a business — a mission.

We believe everyone deserves a clean, safe place to live. That is why giving back is not something we plan to do someday — it is something we are already doing.

Helping People Keep Their Homes

We have cleaned homes for free for disabled individuals facing eviction due to living conditions. When someone is at risk of losing their home over something we can fix, we show up — no invoice, no strings attached.

Supporting Homeless Families

Our long-term vision is to help homeless communities, especially families with children and the most vulnerable. We are building toward that goal with every job we take, because the work we do today funds the difference we want to make tomorrow.
