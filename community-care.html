Our Mission

Community Care Program

We believe everyone deserves a clean, safe place to live. That's why we offer free and discounted cleanings for elderly individuals, disabled people, veterans, and those facing hardship in Mount Vernon, Burlington, and the other towns on our route. Giving back isn't something we plan to do someday — it's something we're already doing.

Who We Help

Elderly Individuals

We provide free or deeply discounted cleanings for seniors on fixed incomes who may struggle with physical maintenance of their homes.

Disabled Individuals

For those with disabilities that make cleaning difficult or impossible, we step in to help keep their homes safe and livable.

Veterans

We honor those who served by offering free cleanings to veterans facing housing instability or eviction due to unclean conditions.

Those Facing Hardship

If you're experiencing financial hardship, homelessness, or facing eviction, we're here to help you maintain a clean, dignified home.

Helping Unhoused Neighbors

A portion of our profits goes to helping unhoused neighbors and families in our community. That help looks like:

  • Care packages and hygiene kits
  • Hot meals
  • Emergency motel stays
  • Blankets and cold-weather gear
  • Tents

Every job you book with us helps make it possible.
