New floors, installed carefully and left clean.

Luxury vinyl plank (LVP) and laminate are popular for good reason: they look like wood, hold up to daily life, and LVP handles kitchens, baths, and entryways well. A good result comes down to prep, layout, and clean transitions.

We remove the old flooring, prep the floor, install the new planks, and put baseboards and trim back in place. Since we also paint and clean, we can freshen the trim and leave the room ready to use.

Flooring FAQs

A few things people usually ask before requesting a quote.

Can you remove my old carpet or flooring?

Yes. Removal can be part of the same job, so the new floor goes in on a clean, prepped surface.

What should I send for a quote?

The rooms, rough square footage, what's on the floor now, and photos. If you've already picked a product, include that too.

Can you paint the trim while you're here?

Yes. Since baseboards come off for the install anyway, it's a good time to paint trim or refresh the room.
