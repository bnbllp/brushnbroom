How we handle what you send us

Quote requests

The form on our homepage asks for your name, email, phone, the service you need, a short description, and optional photos. That information is delivered to us through Formspree so we can reply about your job. We do not sell it, and we do not use it for a mailing list.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics to see which pages get visits. That can include your browser, general location, and the pages you open. You can block it with your browser settings.
